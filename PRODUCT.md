# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Affluent homeowners, developers, and design-literate clients commissioning high-end residential architecture and landscape work. They arrive curious or already in conversation, often on a large screen, in an unhurried browsing mood. Their job: judge whether this studio's body of work and taste warrant a conversation. They read fluency in the drawings themselves, not in marketing claims.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio site that puts twenty years of project drawings (plans, sections, elevations, site and landscape studies) at the center and lets the archive speak. Success is an immersive browse: a visitor moves through the work, opens project after project, and leaves with a clear sense of the studio's range and rigor. Enquiry is the secondary action, available but never pushy. The drawings are the argument.

## Brand Personality

Bold, architectural, dramatic. Confident and authored, with strong typographic presence and high contrast. Voice is spare and exact, the way a draftsperson annotates a sheet: specific nouns, real dimensions, no salesmanship. Dramatic through scale, composition, and the precision of the linework, not through ornament or noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / AI template: card grids, purple gradients, tiny tracked eyebrows, hero-metric blocks, identical icon-heading-text tiles.
- Cluttered real-estate / agency sites: stock photography, badges, dense listings, loud repeated CTAs.
- Trendy brutalist / Y2K: neon, chaotic grids, novelty fonts, trend-chasing that dates within a year.
- Corporate / sterile: safe, stocky, beige-corporate with no point of view.

## Design Principles

- The drawing is the hero. Real architectural linework carries every surface; photography and decoration stay subordinate or absent.
- Show range, don't claim it. Let breadth and depth of the archive prove the studio rather than adjectives.
- Draftsman's precision. Annotations, dimensions, sheet codes, and grids are authentic to the discipline, not decorative cosplay.
- Drama from scale and contrast. Impact comes from typographic and compositional confidence, not effects.
- Quiet conversion. The path to enquiry is always present and never interrupts the browse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text and annotations must clear 4.5:1 against their backgrounds (watch light blueprint linework on dark plates). Full keyboard support for the portfolio lightbox (Esc, arrow keys, focus management). Honor prefers-reduced-motion with crossfade or instant alternatives for the auto-scroll rail, reveals, and counters. Maintain visible focus states and meaningful alt/aria text for the SVG drawings.
