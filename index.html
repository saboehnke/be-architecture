We design the house and the ground it sits in as one continuous idea.

BE is a small architecture and landscape studio working at the seam between built form and terrain. For twenty years we have drawn high-end homes and the gardens, courts and approaches that hold them, never one without the other.

Our archive is a record of that practice: plans, sections, planting schedules and site studies, kept by hand and refined in the field.
